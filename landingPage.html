Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about the Volunteer Learning Hub

What is the Volunteer Learning Hub?

The Volunteer Learning Hub is designed to help new volunteers prepare for their role at St. Anthony North Hospital. It provides essential information, interactive learning materials, and short assessments to help you feel prepared before your first shift.

Where should I start?

Start with the pre-assessment to see what you already know. Based on your results, you can review learning materials on topics that need additional attention before completing the final assessment.

How long does it take to complete?

Most volunteers can complete the Learning Hub in approximately [X–Y] minutes. You can take your time reviewing any topics that are less familiar to you.

What should I do if I don't pass the post-assessment?

No problem—you can review the learning materials again and revisit topics you may have missed before retaking the assessment.
